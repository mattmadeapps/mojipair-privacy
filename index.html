Users in the UK and EEA may be asked to provide consent for personalised advertising in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

For more information about how Google handles data, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where applicable, processing by advertising providers may rely on:

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Retention

The App itself does not retain personal data. Third-party providers may retain information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data

Requests relating to data processed by Google AdMob should be directed to Google.
